# Recommendation Logic

Plots are prioritized using three signals:
1. Agronomic risk: crop health, irrigation stress, and weather exposure.
2. Financial impact: estimated lost revenue or cost per square metre.
3. Actionability: whether a farmer can respond within the next 24 to 72 hours.

High-priority plots are those with high risk and high estimated financial impact.
Medium-priority plots require monitoring.
Low-priority plots are stable and do not need immediate intervention.
